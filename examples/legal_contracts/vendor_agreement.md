# Master Vendor & Corporate Relationship Agreement

## Section 1: Corporate Structure and Affiliates
Vendor Alpha is an international technology supplier organized under Delaware law. Vendor Alpha wholly owns Subsidiary Beta, its primary European software and intellectual property division. Subsidiary Beta manages all software asset portfolios for the group and serves as the operational holding entity.

## Section 2: Intellectual Property Assets
Subsidiary Beta holds Patent 9821, covering distributed consensus and property graph indexing algorithms. All research and developments generated by Subsidiary Beta are assigned to Patent 9821 under European jurisdiction.

## Section 3: Liability Caps and Indemnification
Section 3.1 states that Vendor Alpha and Subsidiary Beta agreed to a liability cap of $5,000,000 for any direct damages. Neither party shall be liable for indirect, punitive, or consequential damages arising prior to the 2023 restructuring.
