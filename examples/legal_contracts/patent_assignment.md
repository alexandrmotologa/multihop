# Intellectual Property Transfer and Assignment Agreement

## Article 1: Assignment of Rights
This Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement confirms that Subsidiary Beta transferred Patent 9821 to Acme Holdings. The assignment includes all global rights, priority claims, and enforcement entitlements.

## Article 2: Governing Terms and Precedence
Clause 7.2 affirms that this transfer precedes the 2023 corporate merger between Acme Holdings and European Partners. Subsidiary Beta warrants that Patent 9821 is unencumbered by third-party liens or security pledges.

## Article 3: Operational License
Acme Holdings grants a non-exclusive operational license to European Division to utilize algorithms embodied in Patent 9821 across all telecommunication networks.
